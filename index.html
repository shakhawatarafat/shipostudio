Good to know

A few things
worth knowing.

Have a specific question about your store? Tell us what you're working on and we'll take it from there.

Can you work with an existing Shopify store? +

Absolutely. We work on existing Shopify stores as well as new builds, from small design updates and custom sections to larger storefront improvements.

Do you develop custom Shopify apps? +

Yes. Our work includes Shopify app development and custom features, including integrations and merchant tools tailored to individual requirements.

Can you redesign a site without starting over? +

Yes. We can often improve the look, layout, user journey, and functionality of your existing theme without replacing the entire storefront.

Do you work with clients outside your country? +

Yes. SHIPO Studio collaborates remotely with businesses around the world and can coordinate around international time zones.

How do we start a project? +

Send us a message with your store URL, a brief description of what you need, and any relevant examples. We can discuss the scope and next steps from there.
