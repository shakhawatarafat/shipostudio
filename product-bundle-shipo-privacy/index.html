Shipo Product Bundles

Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Shipo Product Bundles processes information when merchants install and use the app on their Shopify stores.

Last updated: August 31, 2026

1. About this policy

Shipo Product Bundles is a Shopify application developed and operated by Shipo Studio. The app allows merchants to create quantity-based product bundle offers, configure percentage discounts, and display bundle options on their online stores.

This policy describes the information the app processes, why it is processed, and how it is handled.

2. Information we process

The app processes only information reasonably required to provide its bundle functionality, merchant administration, discount calculation, billing attribution, and Shopify compliance requirements.

This may include:

  • Shopify shop identifiers and basic store information.
  • Product identifiers, product titles, variants, collections, and bundle configuration.
  • Order and line-item information required to identify purchases generated through app-created bundle offers.
  • Refund information required to adjust usage-based billing when bundle-related order amounts are refunded.
  • App installation and permission information supplied by Shopify.
Customer identification data: Shipo Product Bundles does not request access to customer names, email addresses, phone numbers, or postal addresses for its normal functionality.

3. How information is used

Information is processed to:

  • Create, edit, enable, disable, and remove bundle offers.
  • Display configured bundle options on applicable product pages.
  • Apply configured quantity discounts through Shopify.
  • Identify revenue associated with app-created bundle purchases for usage-based Shopify billing.
  • Process refund adjustments associated with previously reported bundle revenue.
  • Maintain the security, reliability, and operation of the app.
  • Respond to mandatory Shopify privacy and compliance requests.

4. Data storage

Bundle configuration is stored using Shopify-managed application data and app-owned product metafields.

Shipo Product Bundles does not maintain a separate merchant database for storing bundle configurations or customer profiles.

The application server is hosted using third-party cloud infrastructure required to operate the application. Information transmitted to the application is processed only as necessary to provide the features described in this policy.

5. Shopify billing

App subscription and usage charges are processed through Shopify's billing system.

For usage-based billing, the app processes bundle-related paid order information to calculate applicable bundle revenue. When qualifying amounts are refunded, the app may submit a corresponding negative usage adjustment.

6. Shopify privacy requests

The app supports Shopify's mandatory privacy and compliance webhooks, including:

  • Customer data requests
  • Customer data redaction requests
  • Shop data redaction requests

Verified requests are processed in accordance with Shopify's requirements and applicable privacy obligations.

7. Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect information processed by the app.

Shopify webhook requests are authenticated using HMAC signature verification before their contents are processed.

Application credentials and secrets are stored using protected server-side environment configuration and are not exposed through the storefront.

8. Data sharing

We do not sell merchant or customer personal information.

Information may be processed by service providers used to operate the application, such as application hosting infrastructure, or by Shopify where necessary to provide Shopify platform functionality and billing.

9. Data retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the application, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, maintain security, and support legitimate accounting or billing requirements.

Shopify-managed application data may also be removed or become unavailable when the app is uninstalled or when Shopify processes applicable privacy requests.

10. Merchant responsibilities

Merchants remain responsible for their own privacy policies and for informing their customers about data processing performed through their Shopify stores where required by applicable law.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app, applicable laws, or Shopify platform requirements change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
