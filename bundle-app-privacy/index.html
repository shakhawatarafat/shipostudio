Collection Bundle Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Collection Bundle, operated by Shipo Studio, handles information when merchants install and use the Collection Bundle application.

1. Information the app accesses

Collection Bundle only requests access required to provide its bundle and discount functionality.

The app may access:

  • Store information such as the store domain.
  • Product and collection information needed to display bundle products.
  • Product variant information used by the bundle selector.
  • Discount configuration created or managed by the app.
  • App configuration such as discount percentage and required item count.

The app does not request access to customer names, customer email addresses, payment information, or customer addresses through the Shopify Admin API.

2. How information is used

Information is used only to provide and maintain Collection Bundle functionality, including:

  • Displaying products and variants in bundle offers.
  • Saving merchant bundle settings.
  • Creating and managing automatic bundle discounts.
  • Authenticating the app with the merchant's store.
  • Maintaining and improving the reliability of the app.

3. Shopify authentication

Collection Bundle uses Shopify's authentication and authorization systems to securely connect the app to a merchant's store. Authentication information is used only for providing the app's functionality.

4. Data sharing

We do not sell merchant or customer information.

Information may be processed by infrastructure providers strictly as necessary to host and operate the application.

5. Data retention

App-related information is retained only for as long as necessary to provide the service, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and maintain security.

When Collection Bundle is uninstalled, app-specific authentication and session information associated with the store may be deleted or scheduled for deletion.

6. Privacy requests and deletion

Collection Bundle supports Shopify's required privacy and data deletion requests. We process applicable privacy requests received through Shopify's privacy webhooks.

Merchants may also contact us directly regarding access, correction, or deletion of information associated with their store.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect information handled by the application from unauthorized access, misuse, alteration, or disclosure.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the application, legal requirements, or our data practices change. The latest version will always be available on this page.
